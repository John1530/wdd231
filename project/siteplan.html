AeroVision Site Plan

This planning document defines the content, visual direction, and responsive structure for the AeroVision Aerospace Learning Hub.

Site Name

AeroVision – Aerospace Learning Hub

The name combines aerospace with vision. It represents a learning platform that helps students and beginners explore flight, aircraft technology, and the future of aerospace engineering.

Optional domain: aerovision-hub.org

Site Purpose

AeroVision will provide an educational hub for students, beginners, and aerospace enthusiasts. The website will explain essential aerospace concepts, introduce aircraft categories, provide interactive learning tools, and present emerging technologies such as drones, computational fluid dynamics, artificial intelligence, and sustainable aviation.

Scenarios

  • How do lift, drag, thrust, and weight affect an aircraft during flight?
  • What types of aircraft are used for commercial, military, research, and unmanned operations?
  • How can I calculate the lift generated by an aircraft wing?
  • What skills and technologies should I learn for a career in aerospace engineering?
